# Mollie Terminal for WooCommerce 0.5.0

This release adds optional iDEAL and Bancontact QR payments to the checkout
panel. It is intended for customers who cannot or do not want to use NFC: the
cashier shows the QR code on screen and the customer scans it with their banking
app.

## Added — QR code payments

- **Choose the payment channel at checkout.** When at least one QR method is
  enabled, cashiers can switch between **Terminal** and **QR code** before
  starting the payment.
- **iDEAL and Bancontact.** Merchants select either or both methods under
  **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments → Mollie Terminal → QR code payments**.
- **Automatic confirmation.** QR payments use the existing Mollie poll and
  webhook flow, so the order finishes automatically after Mollie confirms it.
- **Reload recovery.** Reloading an order with an open QR attempt restores the
  QR channel and resumes polling.
- **Test-mode option.** iDEAL QR works with a Mollie test API key and can be
  tried without a terminal. Bancontact QR requires live mode.

## Payment verification

Payment attempts now record their Mollie method. Reconciliation looks the
payment up in the order's attempt history and rejects a response whose method
does not match what was recorded, or whose payment ID this order never created.
Attempts recorded before 0.5.0 (no stored method) accept `pointofsale` plus the
QR methods enabled in the settings. The existing per-order payment
lock and Mollie-as-source-of-truth model are unchanged.

## Upgrade

No configuration changes are required. QR payments are off by default. Enable
iDEAL and/or Bancontact in the gateway settings to show the channel switch at
checkout. After payment on their phone, customers land on the standard
WooCommerce order-received page.
